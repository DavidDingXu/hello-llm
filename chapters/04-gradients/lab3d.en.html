Calculation, update log and notes

The input stays at 2 and the target at 6. The ground axes are parameters w and b; height is the same squared loss. The valley satisfies 2w+b=6, so one problem cannot uniquely determine both parameters.

Only the endpoints perform a parameter update. The green path samples 64 intervals along the straight parameter segment between old and new values, recalculating L at every sample. It shows loss while crossing the valley; it is not a straight line between endpoint losses. The yellow arrow shows the direction of −∇L in the parameter plane, with its length scaled for display.

From w=1, b=0, rate 0.1 gives L=0.64 for w only, or L=0 for both. Rate 0.3 gives L=31.36 for w only, or L=64 for both. An oversized step crosses the valley. Both derivatives are calculated at the same old point.
